# Cadence

Cadence is a course platform. Learners buy Courses, watch Lessons, and can ask for their money back within limits.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get back the money they paid for a Purchase, which the team reviews by hand. An approved Refund returns the money and ends the learner's access to the Course. A Purchase has at most one Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback, pending chargeback

**Refund amount**:
What an Approved Refund gives back: always the full amount the learner paid for the Purchase, never the Course's list price and never part of it.
_Avoid_: Partial refund, refundable amount

**Requested**:
The state of a Refund the team has not reviewed yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, open

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund the team accepted, meaning the money goes back and access ends.

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund the team turned down, meaning nothing is given back and access stays. A Declined Refund is final for that Purchase.

**Refund eligibility**:
The rule that decides whether a Purchase can be refunded: within 14 calendar days of purchase, under 2 hours watched, under half the lessons completed, and not a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, refund window (when meaning the whole rule)

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the cardholder opens with their card issuer to force the money back. Cadence does not model these, and the word never means a Refund.
